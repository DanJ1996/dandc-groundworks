## About (real copy)
D&C Groundworks & Civils Ltd is a family-run groundworks and civil engineering contractor based in Christchurch, delivering domestic and commercial projects across Bournemouth, Poole and the wider Dorset region. Founded on over a decade of hands-on industry experience, we provide a complete groundworks service — from site clearance and excavation through to foundations, drainage and finished surfaces. A significant proportion of our work comes through referrals and returning clients who value our quality, transparent pricing and dependable delivery.

## Three pillars
- **Relationships** — Much of our work comes through word of mouth, and that's no accident. We build lasting relationships with our clients through honest advice, clear communication and a job done right — so they return to us, and recommend us to others.
- **Experience** — With over a decade of hands-on experience across domestic and commercial groundworks, there's little we haven't tackled — from garden dig-outs and drainage to foundations, driveways and full site works.
- **Accountability** — Fully insured and committed to health and safety on every site, we manage each project from first consultation to final handover. You deal directly with the team doing the work — turning up when we say we will, and leaving every site clean and safe.
